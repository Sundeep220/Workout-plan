### BMR (Mifflin-St Jeor — Male)

```
BMR = 10 × weight(kg) + 6.25 × height(cm) - 5 × age + 5
BMR = 10(97.5) + 6.25(183) - 5(25) + 5
BMR = 975 + 1143.75 - 125 + 5
BMR ≈ 2,000 kcal/day
```

### TDEE (Total Daily Energy Expenditure)

| Activity Level | Multiplier | TDEE |
|---------------|-----------|------|
| Sedentary (rest day) | 1.2 | ~2,400 kcal |
| **Your training days (lifting + 30 min cardio, 6x/week)** | **1.55-1.6** | **~3,100-3,200 kcal** |
| **Weighted average (6 train + 1 rest)** | **~1.5** | **~3,000 kcal** |

### Deficit Strategy — 1 Month Hardcore Cut

| | Value |
|--|-------|
| **Average TDEE** | ~3,000 kcal/day |
| **Target intake** | 1,300-1,400 kcal/day |
| **Daily deficit** | ~1,600-1,700 kcal |
| **Weekly deficit** | ~11,200-11,900 kcal |
| **Expected fat loss** | ~1.4-1.5 kg/week (~6 kg in 4 weeks) |

> ⚠️ **This is an EXTREME deficit.** Sustainable for ~4 weeks max with your training load. After 4 weeks, move to a moderate deficit (~500 kcal, ~2,500 kcal/day) or you WILL lose muscle and crash. Signs to stop early: constant fatigue, strength dropping >15%, dizziness, inability to recover.

> 💡 **Protein is your shield.** At this deficit, high protein (140-160g/day) + heavy lifting = muscle preservation. Without it, you'll lose lean mass fast.

### Day 1 — ~1,350 kcal | P: 152g | F: 42g | C: 72g

**Breakfast — Masala Egg Omelette + Oats (310 kcal)**
- 3 whole eggs + 2 egg whites
- 1/4 cup diced onion, tomato, green chilli, coriander
- Cook in 1 tsp olive oil / ghee
- Side: 30g oats cooked in water with pinch of salt

> 🍳 **How to make:**
> 1. Crack 3 whole eggs + 2 egg whites into a bowl. Beat well.
> 2. Add diced onion, tomato, green chilli, coriander, salt, pepper, pinch of turmeric.
> 3. Heat 1 tsp olive oil/ghee in a non-stick pan on medium heat.
> 4. Pour egg mixture. Let it set for 2 min on one side.
> 5. Flip carefully, cook 1-2 min more. Done.
> 6. **Oats:** 30g oats + 100ml water in a microwave-safe bowl. Microwave 2 min. Add pinch of salt. Stir.
>
> 📺 [Masala Omelette](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=indian+masala+omelette+recipe) | [Savoury Oats](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=savoury+oats+recipe+healthy)

**Mid-Morning Snack — Greek Yogurt Bowl (120 kcal)**
- 150g low-fat Greek yogurt (or hung curd)
- 5 almonds (chopped)
- Pinch of cinnamon

> 🥣 **How to make:**
> 1. Scoop 150g Greek yogurt / hung curd into a bowl.
> 2. Chop 5 almonds roughly and sprinkle on top.
> 3. Dust with cinnamon. Eat cold. Zero cooking.
> **Make hung curd at home:** Tie 500g curd in muslin cloth, hang over a bowl for 3-4 hours in fridge. The water drains out → thick, high-protein hung curd.
>
> 📺 [Hung Curd at Home](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=how+to+make+hung+curd+at+home)

**Lunch — Chicken Tikka + Salad + Masala Egg White Fry (400 kcal)**
- 150g chicken breast tikka (marinate in curd, turmeric, chilli powder, garam masala — pan-grill)
- Big cucumber-tomato-onion salad with lemon
- 4 egg whites — **masala pan-fried** (halve boiled whites, pan-sear with chaat masala + cumin)

> 🍗 **How to make:**
> 1. Cut 150g chicken breast into 1-inch cubes.
> 2. Marinate: 2 tbsp curd + 1/2 tsp turmeric + 1 tsp chilli powder + 1/2 tsp garam masala + salt + 1 tsp lemon juice. Mix well.
> 3. Marinate 15 min minimum (overnight is best — keep in fridge).
> 4. Heat a non-stick pan on HIGH. No oil needed.
> 5. Place chicken pieces, cook 4-5 min per side until charred spots appear.
> 6. **Masala egg whites:** Halve 4 boiled egg whites. Pan-sear cut-side down in the same pan 1 min. Sprinkle chaat masala + cumin + black pepper. Crispy and flavourful.
> 7. Squeeze lemon on top. Serve tikka + masala egg whites + big salad.
>
> 📺 [Chicken Tikka on Pan](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=chicken+tikka+tawa+recipe+indian) | [Masala Egg Fry](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=masala+egg+fry+recipe+indian)

**Evening Snack — Protein Shake + Egg White Bhurji (200 kcal)**
- 1 scoop whey protein (30g) in 250ml water
- 4 egg whites — **bhurji style** (scrambled with onion + green chilli)

> 🥤 **How to make:**
> 1. Shake: 250ml cold water + 1 scoop whey. Shake 15 sec.
> 2. **Egg white bhurji:** Heat non-stick pan (no oil). Add pinch of chopped onion + slit green chilli. Toss 30 sec.
> 3. Pour in 4 egg whites. Scramble quickly on medium heat with salt + turmeric + pepper.
> 4. Cook 1-2 min. Quick, flavourful, different from plain boiled.
>
> 📺 [Egg White Bhurji](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=egg+white+bhurji+recipe+healthy)

**Dinner — Paneer Do Pyaza (Light) + Salad (350 kcal)**
- 80g low-fat paneer cubed
- 2 onions (sliced + chopped), 1 tomato, capsicum, ginger-garlic, 1 tsp oil
- Side: raw cucumber sticks

> 🧀 **How to make:**
> 1. Heat 1 tsp oil. Sauté 1 sliced onion till golden brown (4-5 min). Remove half for garnish.
> 2. Add 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste to remaining onion. Cook 1 min.
> 3. Add 1 chopped tomato + 1/2 chopped capsicum. Cook till mushy (3 min).
> 4. Add 1/4 tsp turmeric + 1/2 tsp garam masala + 1/2 tsp coriander powder + salt. Stir.
> 5. Add 80g cubed low-fat paneer. Toss gently 2-3 min.
> 6. Add the reserved fried onions on top + squeeze of lemon.
> 7. Serve with cucumber sticks. "Do pyaza" = double onion = double flavour.
>
> 📺 [Paneer Do Pyaza](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=paneer+do+pyaza+recipe+easy) | [Low Calorie Paneer Curry](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=healthy+paneer+curry+low+calorie+recipe)

### Day 2 — ~1,340 kcal | P: 158g | F: 38g | C: 74g

> 5. Add 1/2 tsp turmeric + 1 tsp coriander powder + 1/2 tsp chilli + 1/2 tsp garam masala + salt.
> 6. Add chicken pieces. Mix well. Cover and cook on LOW heat 15 min.

**Dinner — Moong Dal + Cucumber Raita (330 kcal)**
- 1/2 cup dry moong dal (cooked — yellow dal)
- Tadka: 1/2 tsp ghee, cumin, garlic, curry leaves
- Raita: 100g curd + grated cucumber + roasted cumin + salt

> 🥣 **How to make:**
> 1. Wash 1/2 cup moong dal. Add to pressure cooker with 2 cups water + 1/4 tsp turmeric + salt.
> 2. Pressure cook 3 whistles. Open when pressure drops. Mash slightly.
> 3. **Tadka:** Heat 1/2 tsp ghee in small pan. Add 1/2 tsp cumin seeds — let them splutter.
> 4. Add 2 chopped garlic cloves + 5-6 curry leaves. Sauté 30 sec.
> 5. Pour tadka over dal. Mix. Add water if too thick.
> 6. **Raita:** Mix 100g curd + grated cucumber + 1/4 tsp roasted cumin + salt in a bowl. Serve cold.
>

**Dinner — Soya Chunk + Egg White Stir-Fry (380 kcal)**
- 50g dry soya chunks (boil, squeeze water)
- Stir-fry with capsicum, onion, soy sauce, chilli flakes, 1 tsp oil
- 4 egg whites — **stir-fried together** (chopped and tossed into the stir-fry)

> 🍲 **How to make:**
> 1. Boil 50g dry soya chunks in hot water for 5 min. Drain. Squeeze out ALL water (this is key — soggy soya = bad taste).

> 3. Heat 1 tsp oil in a wok/pan on HIGH heat.
> 4. Add sliced onion + sliced capsicum. Toss 2 min.
> 5. Add squeezed soya chunks. Stir-fry 2 min.
> 6. Add 1 tbsp soy sauce + 1/2 tsp chilli flakes + salt. Toss on high heat for 2 min.
> 7. Toss in chopped egg whites. Stir-fry 1 min more — they absorb the soy sauce flavour.
> **Key:** HIGH heat. Don't crowd the pan. The egg whites become part of the dish, not a boring side.
>

## Critical Rules for the 4-Week Cut

1. **Weigh yourself daily** — same time (morning, after bathroom, before food). Track the weekly AVERAGE, not daily fluctuations.
2. **Drink 3-4 liters of water/day** — appetite control + performance. Add electrolytes (salt + lime water).
3. **Sleep 7-8 hours** — non-negotiable. Sleep debt = cortisol = muscle loss + fat retention.
4. **Don't skip protein** — if you miss a meal, at least have a protein shake. Protein is the #1 priority.
5. **Black coffee before workouts** — caffeine is free energy on a deficit. 200mg (2 cups) 30 min pre-workout.
6. **Repeat this 14-day cycle** for Week 3 and Week 4. After 4 weeks, reassess.
7. **If strength drops >15% or you feel sick** — add 200-300 kcal (from carbs). Health > speed.

## After the 4-Week Cut — Reverse Diet Plan

After 4 weeks of aggressive deficit, **DO NOT** jump back to normal eating. Reverse slowly:

| Week | Daily Calories | Notes |
|------|---------------|-------|
| **Week 5** | 1,600 kcal | Add 200 kcal (extra carbs around workout) |
| **Week 6** | 1,800 kcal | Add another 200 kcal |
| **Week 7** | 2,000 kcal | Close to BMR now |
| **Week 8+** | 2,200-2,500 kcal | Moderate deficit or maintenance — sustainable long-term |

> After 4 weeks you should be ~91-92 kg. Continue at moderate deficit (2,200-2,500 kcal) to reach 80-84 kg goal over the next 3-4 months without burning out.
