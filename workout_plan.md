# Workout Plan — Fat Loss + Muscle Gain

**Current:** 97-98 kg | 6 ft | 27% body fat  
**Goal:** 80-84 kg | Build muscle | Lose fat  
**Cycle:** 6 days on, 1 day rest, repeat

---

## How to Read This Plan

- **Sets x Reps** — e.g., 4x8-10 means 4 sets of 8 to 10 reps
- **Rest between sets** — 60-90 sec for compound lifts, 45-60 sec for isolation
- **Tempo** — 2 sec lowering, 1 sec pause, 1 sec lifting (controlled reps)
- **Progressive overload** — When you hit the top of the rep range on ALL sets, increase weight next session (+2.5 kg upper, +5 kg lower)
- **(B)** = Bilateral — both arms/legs work together (barbell, machine)
- **(U)** = Unilateral — each arm/leg works independently (dumbbell, single-arm/leg). **Fixes muscle imbalance.**

> **Muscle Balance Rule:** Every muscle group gets BOTH a (B) compound AND a (U) dumbbell/single-arm exercise.
> On (U) exercises, always start with your **weaker side first** and match the same reps on the stronger side (don't do extra).

### Session Flow (every training day)

| Step | What | Time |
|------|------|------|
| 1 | Warm-up (dynamic stretches) | ~5-8 min |
| 2 | Lifting (6 exercises) | ~55 min |
| 3 | Core / Abs Finisher (3 exercises) | ~8-10 min |
| 4 | Cardio | ~30 min |
| 5 | Cool-Down Stretching | ~5-8 min |

> **Cardio note:** Available at Cult — Treadmill, Cross Trainer, Dance Classes. If you attend a dance class on a training day, skip the 30 min post-lifting cardio — the class covers it. Plan dance on push or pull days to avoid doubling up on leg fatigue. Dance on rest day = active recovery.

> **Core progression:** Start with bodyweight core days only. Once you can complete all sets cleanly, start adding the weighted core days. Eventually do all 6 core routines per cycle.

## Key Rules

1. **Log your weights.** Track every session. No guessing.
2. **Progressive overload** — Hit top of rep range on all sets → add weight next time.
3. **If you miss a day** — Pick up where you left off. Don't skip, shift.
4. **Cycle repeats every 7 days.** Doesn't matter which calendar day you start on.
5. **Protein target** — 1.8-2g per kg bodyweight = ~175-195g protein per day.
6. **Caloric deficit** — Aim for 300-500 cal deficit. At 27% BF, you will build muscle even in a deficit.

## Home Pelvic / Hip Mobility Routine (~10-15 min, no equipment)

Do this **daily at home** — morning or evening. Improves blood flow, pelvic mobility, hip flexibility, and posture. All bodyweight, no gym needed.

### Pelvic Floor Exercises

| # | Exercise | How To | Duration / Reps |
|---|----------|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | [**Kegels**](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=how+to+do+kegels+for+men+proper+form) | Squeeze pelvic floor muscles (like stopping pee mid-stream). Hold tight, then release. | 3 x 10 holds (5 sec hold, 5 sec relax) |
| 2 | [**Reverse Kegels**](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=how+to+reverse+kegel+men+form) | Gently push/relax the pelvic floor (opposite of Kegel — like bearing down lightly). Focus on releasing tension. | 3 x 10 (5 sec push, 5 sec relax) |
| 3 | [**Pelvic Tilts (Lying)**](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=pelvic+tilts+lying+down+form) | Lie on back, knees bent, feet flat. Tilt pelvis up by flattening lower back into the floor, then arch slightly. Slow rhythm. | 3 x 15 |
| 4 | [**Glute Bridge Hold**](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=glute+bridge+hold+proper+form) | Lie on back, knees bent. Push hips up, squeeze glutes and pelvic floor at top. Hold. | 3 x 20 sec hold |

### Hip Mobility & Blood Flow

| # | Exercise | How To | Duration / Reps |
|---|----------|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | [**90/90 Hip Stretch**](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=90+90+hip+stretch+proper+form) | Sit on floor, front leg bent 90° in front, back leg bent 90° behind. Lean forward gently over front shin. Switch sides. | 2 x 30 sec each side |
| 2 | [**Deep Squat Hold (Malasana)**](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=deep+squat+hold+malasana+mobility) | Squat all the way down, feet slightly wider than hips, elbows pressing knees out. Hold and breathe. | 2 x 30-45 sec |
| 3 | [**Hip Circles (Standing)**](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=standing+hip+circles+exercise) | Stand on one leg, lift other knee to hip height, make slow circles outward then inward. | 2 x 10 each direction, each leg |
| 4 | [**Supine Figure-4 Stretch**](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=supine+figure+4+stretch+form) | Lie on back, cross one ankle over opposite knee. Pull bottom knee toward chest. Hold. | 2 x 30 sec each side |
| 5 | [**Cat-Cow (on all fours)**](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=cat+cow+stretch+form) | Alternate between arching back (cow) and rounding back (cat). Move with breath. | 2 x 10 slow reps |
| 6 | [**Happy Baby Pose**](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=happy+baby+pose+stretch) | Lie on back, grab outside of feet, pull knees toward armpits. Rock side to side gently. | 1 x 45-60 sec |

> **When to do this:** Every day, even on rest days. Best done in the morning to wake up hips, or at night before sleep to decompress. Can also do it as a quick pre-workout warm-up on leg days.
