Workout Plan — Fat Loss + Muscle Gain

Current: 97-98 kg | 6 ft | 27% body fat
Goal: 80-84 kg | Build muscle | Lose fat
Cycle: 6 days on, 1 day rest, repeat

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

HOW TO READ THIS PLAN

• Sets x Reps — e.g., 4x8-10 means 4 sets of 8 to 10 reps
• Rest between sets — 60-90 sec for compound lifts, 45-60 sec for isolation
• Tempo — 2 sec lowering, 1 sec pause, 1 sec lifting (controlled reps)
• Progressive overload — When you hit the top of the rep range on ALL sets, increase weight next session (+2.5 kg upper, +5 kg lower)
• (B) = Bilateral — both arms/legs work together (barbell, machine)
• (U) = Unilateral — each arm/leg works independently (dumbbell, single-arm/leg). Fixes muscle imbalance.

⚠️ Muscle Balance Rule: Every muscle group gets BOTH a (B) compound AND a (U) dumbbell/single-arm exercise.
On (U) exercises, always start with your weaker side first and match the same reps on the stronger side (don't do extra).

SESSION FLOW (every training day)
1. Warm-up (dynamic stretches) — ~5-8 min
2. Lifting (6 exercises) — ~55 min
3. Core / Abs Finisher (3 exercises) — ~8-10 min
4. Cardio — ~30 min
5. Cool-Down Stretching (on mat) — ~5-8 min

💡 Cardio note: Available at Cult — Treadmill, Cross Trainer, Dance Classes. If you attend a dance class on a training day, skip the 30 min post-lifting cardio — the class covers it. Plan dance on push or pull days to avoid doubling up on leg fatigue. Dance on rest day = active recovery.

💡 Core progression: Start with bodyweight core days only. Once you can complete all sets cleanly, start adding the weighted core days. Eventually do all 6 core routines per cycle.

KEY RULES

1. Log your weights. Track every session. No guessing.
2. Progressive overload — Hit top of rep range on all sets → add weight next time.
3. If you miss a day — Pick up where you left off. Don't skip, shift.
4. Cycle repeats every 7 days. Doesn't matter which calendar day you start on.
5. Protein target — 1.8-2g per kg bodyweight = ~175-195g protein per day.
6. Caloric deficit — Aim for 300-500 cal deficit. At 27% BF, you will build muscle even in a deficit.

HOME PELVIC / HIP MOBILITY ROUTINE (~10-15 min, no equipment)

Do this daily at home — morning or evening. Improves blood flow, pelvic mobility, hip flexibility, and posture. All bodyweight, no gym needed.

PELVIC FLOOR EXERCISES

1. Kegels — Squeeze pelvic floor muscles (like stopping pee mid-stream). Hold tight, then release. 3 x 10 holds (5 sec hold, 5 sec relax)
   ▶️ https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=how+to+do+kegels+for+men+proper+form

2. Reverse Kegels — Gently push/relax the pelvic floor (opposite of Kegel). Focus on releasing tension. 3 x 10 (5 sec push, 5 sec relax)
   ▶️ https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=how+to+reverse+kegel+men+form

3. Pelvic Tilts (Lying) — Lie on back, knees bent, feet flat. Tilt pelvis up by flattening lower back into the floor, then arch slightly. 3 x 15
   ▶️ https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=pelvic+tilts+lying+down+form

4. Glute Bridge Hold — Lie on back, knees bent. Push hips up, squeeze glutes and pelvic floor at top. 3 x 20 sec hold
   ▶️ https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=glute+bridge+hold+proper+form
